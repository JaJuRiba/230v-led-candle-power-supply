# P1 sample production acceptance — NOT RUN

Hold the lot until the responsible engineer signs the limits sheet for this exact PCB, adapter, jumper configuration, harness and enclosure revision. A CAD review is not a powered test. Record lot/serial, firmware if applicable, instruments/calibration, fixture, ambient, input at J1, loading and sample size. Quarantine failures and investigate before rework/retest; no substitution by visual similarity.

|Stage|Procedure and disposition|Record|
|---|---|---|
|Incoming, every unit|Compare outline/thickness, finish, rev and keying to drawing. Inspect solder bridges, connector anchors, DNPs, regulator tab, Q1, OVP, branch switches and polarized capacitor against assembly drawings. Verify correct paired selector links by continuity. Reject wrong population, damage or shorts before power.|NOT RUN|
|Harness, every unit|Unplug adapter. Verify end-to-end polarity and return for each of five outputs and no unintended cross-connections. Mate/unmate, inspect latch, strain relief and retention. Dry-fit board and closed lid: no tab/fastener contact, crushed wires or forced bends.|NOT RUN|
|Power-up, every unit|Use isolated current-limited DC bench supply and dummy loads only. Begin at low current; stop on unexpected current, rail or heating. Measure all outputs unloaded, then individually at approved reference load. Check voltage at cable end and at PCB. Power down and discharge before changing links.|NOT RUN|
|First articles, both approved setpoints|Test at approved minimum/nominal/maximum J1 voltage, no load, one branch and all five at 20 and 50 mA each. Use electronic loads or rated resistors: 150/60 Ω at 3 V; 225/90 Ω at 4.5 V, ≥0.5 W each. Scope startup, switch-off, load steps and repeated restarts. Compare peak and steady voltage/current to signed P1 limits, including healthy outputs.|NOT RUN|
|Selective protection, engineering sample|With bounded source energy and four healthy loads at 50 mA, apply a controlled brief fault to each remaining output in turn. Log peak vs steady fault current, healthy minimum voltage, source limiting, temperature, recovery and resets. Verify mode-specific OVP trip and load-side peak with approved isolated DC fault-injection fixture, no valuable loads. Stop on a missed trip or if safe test energy/threshold is undefined.|NOT RUN|
|Thermal and materials, engineering sample|At approved input corners, both modes, max continuous loading, close actual enclosure with production harness. Record ambient, elapsed time, stabilized LDO/tab, switch, connector and nearest plastic temperatures; compare with signed junction estimates, derating and material limits. Recheck latch/fastener creep and cable strain after cooling. No pass from open-board temperature alone.|NOT RUN|
|System first article|After SELV evidence and DC tests, connect the exact sealed adapter to the approved harness. Repeat worst cases with representative candles (cells removed, polarity verified); capture restart/timer and upstream switching behavior. No live mains probing. Hold if any load rating or limit is unknown.|NOT RUN|

One record per unit, mode and case: `serial, rev, mode, VIN min/max, load vector, channel, Vout DC/min/peak at load, Iin, Ifault peak/steady, healthy Vmin, temperatures, trip/recovery, signed limits/revision, trace/photo, pass/fail, operator/date`. For production, define lot size and sample plan before building: routine unpowered/polarity/functional screens on every unit; repeat engineering stress tests on first article and after component, adapter, enclosure or process changes. An installation or safety approval requires its own review.
