# External adapter shortlist — neither qualified nor ordered

Prefer the fixed Europlug for the first P1 evaluation: one less mating interface. Keep the complete adapter outside the controller and wall cavity. Only its isolated DC cable goes to the controller. Never connect AC to J1. The choices below are candidates for a SELV supply, not a declaration that the delivered unit or finished system meets a particular safety standard.

|Manufacturer ordering code | 230 V / 50 Hz input | Regulated DC at plug | Temperature (ambient, derate per curve) |
|---|---|---|---|
|[MEAN WELL GSM06E06-P1J](https://www.meanwell.com/Upload/PDF/GSM06E/GSM06E-SPEC.PDF), integral 2-pin Europlug | 85–264 VAC, 47–63 Hz | 6 V, 1 A, 6 W; ±5.0% = **5.70–6.30 V**; 50 mV p-p max ripple/noise | 0 to +50 °C |
|[MEAN WELL GEM06I06-P1J](https://www.meanwell.com/Upload/PDF/GEM06I/GEM06I-SPEC.PDF) **plus AC plug-EU2** (sold separately) | 80–264 VAC, 47–63 Hz | 6 V, 1 A, 6 W; ±5.0% = **5.70–6.30 V**; 50 mV p-p max ripple/noise | −20 to +70 °C |

Source: each linked manufacturer specification, 6 V column, notes, derating curve and connector drawing. P1J is 5.5 mm OD × 2.1 mm ID × 11 mm, centre positive. Specify a matching insulated female socket-to-keyed-J1 harness, strain relief, exact pin mapping and rated contacts; the barrel does **not** plug into JST SH. Meter the assembled harness before connecting it.

**What ±5% means.** The sheets set DC at the plug terminal at 50% load; tolerance includes setup, line and load regulation, not a further ±5% to add for each. Check test conditions (including light load) and ripple separately. Neither spec guarantees those endpoints at the *board* after an extra harness, connector, reverse-protection drop and startup transient. Scope at the plug, J1, regulator input and farthest output; verify cold start, no load, five loads, single fault, repeated upstream switching and recovery. Sweep below the low DC corner by the measured worst drop/ripple for dropout; above the high corner for OVP and heat. The 1 A nameplate is capacity, **not** an adjustable 0.40 A protective limit. Neither is a drop-in E1 source (E1 requires 6.05–6.15 V including ripple at J1).

**Material and approval gates.** Both sheets describe a Class II unit, primary–secondary 2 × MOPP and a 94V-0 plastic *adapter case*. These are manufacturer statements, not independent verification of a particular shipment's SELV status. Before selection obtain the current model-specific EU Declaration of Conformity, safety/isolation documentation covering the exact suffix and plug, and confirmation of suitability for the intended use. Check delivered markings, provenance, condition and country plug. No approval or flame rating transfers to the PCB, cable, connector or printed housing. Specify exact filament grade and print/process; obtain thermal/flammability data for actual thickness and orientation or mark unknown. No generic PLA/PETG temperature or flame claim. Test retention/creep under screw and cable load at the hottest measured wall/boss, with margin to material limits; never qualify mains insulation by printing a box. Use the adapter in open air within its derating envelope. Qualify the *separate* closed DC controller at 35 °C still air or its approved higher design ambient; measure LDO/tab, switches, connector and nearby polymer after stabilization. Stop on excessive temperature, odor, deformation, cycling or loss of regulation.
