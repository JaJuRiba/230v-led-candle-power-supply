## Power and fault budgets

Specify a **separately qualified regulated isolated 6 V SELV adapter**, 1 A class. J1 operating envelope **5.70–6.30 V at the pins including ripple and leads**, no-load to load; do not substitute an unregulated wall wart. Example sourcing family: [GlobTek GTM41134-06VV-Q](https://www.globtek.com/ds/medical-ict-ite-household-use-power-supply/wall-plug-in/Xhu9OyH7tBZ/Ud5MWJvTD-Q/gtm41134-06vv-q.html), specify 6.0 V/1 A, regional plug and certified configuration. Family approvals are not approval of a particular adapter/harness; require certificate, exact order code and measured tolerance/start/stop behavior. A ±5% DC tolerance **plus** separate ripple/overshoot is not automatically within contract. TPS2553 switches operate only to 6.5 V even in a regulator pass fault. No surge suppressor or input overvoltage disconnect. Initially use isolated, current-limited bench DC (50 mA unloaded; 0.45 A when loading).

* **Q1 AO3401A** G1=return, S2=protected VIN, D3=raw positive. Body diode conducts D→S at correct polarity, channel then enhances; reversed J1 leaves it off. Not reverse-current isolation with externally driven outputs. At 0.4 A, 60 mΩ at −4.5 V gives 24 mV at datasheet test temperature; budget **60 mV total** to U1 including copper, contact variation and hot RDS(on), verify in situ.
* **U1 LT1963AEQ#TRPBF**, adjustable E-grade DDPAK, **1 SHDN tied high, 2 IN, 3 GND and tab, 4 OUT, 5 ADJ**. Q package, not E1's live-tab 1117. C1 local 1 µF, C2 positive pin 1 at OUT, 22 µF 16 V tantalum (19.8 µF initial min); local C3–C7 are extra upstream ceramics. Confirm actual impedance, low-temperature ESR, biased capacitance and load-step stability. At minimum source and guarded high maximum, 5.70−0.060−4.659 = **0.981 V** vs 0.35 V maximum dropout at 0.5 A; even the 1.5 A maximum 0.55 V leaves margin. Nominal headroom isn't the criterion.
* Top feedback R1=1.47 kΩ; lower R2=1.00 kΩ permanently connected. JP1 closes R3=1.18 kΩ to GND in parallel with R2. Divider draws ≥1.17 mA, above 1 mA reference test floor even with open outputs. ADJ bias flows into pin. R9 samples pre-switch rail on VSEL copper independently of ADJ; R10 permanent ground return. R11=**17.4 kΩ**, changed from study's 18.2 kΩ: original high minimum release around 4.653 V can be below this regulator's normal upper corner. JP2 closes independent OVP lower leg. No fault output is fed back into U1 regulation.
* **U7 TPS3700DDCR**: 1 OUTA NC, 2 GND, 3 INA+ GND, 4 INB− midpoint, 5 VDD protected input with C8 100 nF, 6 OUTB open-drain BR_EN. R12 10 kΩ pulls BR_EN to VSEL. **U2–U6 TPS2553DBVR**: 1 IN=VSEL, 2 GND, 3 EN=BR_EN (not tied to IN), 4 FAULT NC, 5 ILIM via own 210 kΩ, 6 distinct OUT. DBV max 135 mΩ ⇒ 6.75 mV at 50 mA before wiring. Both TI parts have pin 1 upper-left counterclockwise numbering (DDC thin vs DBV regular; generic six-lead model for U7). Monitor pulls low against <0.65 mA in a bounded pass fault; compare VOL 0.25 V vs EN low 0.66 V. Released EN ≳2.86 V vs high threshold 1.1 V. Measure each EN.

[audit.py](audit.py) enumerates ±0.20% precision resistors (0.1% initial, 25 ppm/K over ±40 K), 0–1 Ω closed link, ADJ 1.174–1.246 V and 0–10 µA bias; TPS3700 rise 396–404 mV, fall 387–400 mV, design allowance ±50 nA sense leakage. DC windows exclude copper and ripple. Minimum normal-to-release margin is only ~39 mV low; qualify in case. Wrong high setting with both bridged is **not detected**. JP1 closed alone disables branches; JP2 closed alone leaves low output with the wrong high threshold. No latching, no discharge, no protection from a shorted switch or failed-low sense. Monitor can float EN high during startup/UVLO. A fast step can reach nearly fault VIN before shutdown. Threshold is **not** a peak-voltage guarantee. See [prior OVP study and bounded stimulus procedure](../../docs/ovp-study/README.md).

**Single short and heat:** 210 kΩ ±1.5% effective gives ~109–153 mA calculated DC limit; budget 154 mA. Four healthy branches + one short = 354 mA; add ≤4 mA divider, switch and detector overhead plus 25 mA regulator ground-current allowance (0.5 A test point) ⇒ **≤383 mA estimated**. Healthy-case budget ≤279 mA input. Limit calculations are not peak-current or fault-selectivity guarantees. Worst low-mode U1 loss ≈1.03 W full load, ≈1.38 W with one short; high-mode full load ≈0.66 W, including 0.158 W ground-current allowance. To keep TJ below 125 °C at 35 °C air requires effective θJA below ~88 °C/W full low, ~65 °C/W in one low-mode short. DDPAK has solid tab-to-front-ground spreader, adjacent stitching, full inner return and back ground copper; do **not** apply datasheet benchmark θJA as measured compact-enclosure performance. High-mode switch short can dissipate ~0.72 W locally; TI DBV benchmark 182.6 °C/W implies ~131 °C rise, so expect thermal cycling. Stop prolonged faults. Check U1, all switches and case to thermal equilibrium with measured ambient; design does not guarantee continuous short survival.

## Acceptance gates still open

Start with dummy loads only; no cells installed. Both modes: no load, 1 and 5 × 20/50 mA, both input extremes, startup, brownout, cable extremes and repeated branch faults. Use ≥0.5 W 60 Ω / 90 Ω resistors for nominal 50 mA low/high or programmable loads. Rotate fault across J2 pairs; record rail, all healthy far-end minima, short peak/DC, reset and temperatures. For OVP fault injection use a separate isolated test fixture or physically isolate U1 OUT before externally driving VSEL; **never parallel two supplies or force a live LDO output**. Capture EN and far-end peaks, pulse duration, automatic restart and decay at several downstream capacitances. Test all four link states. No real candle until its own permitted DC/transient envelope, polarity and impedance needs are known. No thermal, electrical or enclosure bench result claimed.

All populated parts top-side SMT; JP1/JP2 are **open copper features, not placed parts**. Candidate JLC catalog leads: [U1 C459702](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/AnalogDevices-LT1963AEQTRPBF/C459702), [U2–U6 C55266](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/C55266), [U7 C33002](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/C33002), [Q1 C15127](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/C15127). Verify exact MPN, stock, tape variant, rotations, connector mating direction/anchor solder, polarized C2 and U1 stencil/thermal pad. Do not substitute TPS2553-1 or fixed-output LT1963A.
